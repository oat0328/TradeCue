Dark-only. Tight pacing for dashboards and controls, with slightly more generous breathing room on marketing surfaces. Deep navy-black backgrounds, subtly lifted ink-blue surfaces, hairline cyan-tinted borders, one disciplined electric-cyan accent, emerald only for positive market states, amber only for caution, and rose only for risk. Surfaces are matte with restrained glow on active intelligence states; radii are medium and engineered, not pill-heavy.
