TradeCue is a premium AI trading and investing SaaS. It uses a Chevy-style "same engine, different trim" membership model: Scout ($39), Copilot ($129), and Autopilot ($249), each with a 30-day trial experience centered on Copilot. Core modules: Cue Radar, Cue Vision multi-timeframe charts, Fundamental Intelligence, Professor Cue, Cue Hustle, Growth Path, Risk Firewall, CueCrypto 24/7, CueFutures, paper trading first, and an owner/admin operations backend. Webull is planned for stocks/ETFs/options/crypto brokerage/data rails, Tradovate for futures, and Financial Modeling Prep for fundamental news/earnings/ratings. Long-only Green Gate, no-chase behavior, risk limits, educational explanations, and no guaranteed-profit claims are foundational.
